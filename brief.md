# Brief

## Who this site is for, and what they should leave knowing

My website is for restaurant owners seeking a professional, user-friendly website for their business. Visitors should understand how I can help them present their restaurant online and make it easier for customers to explore the menu, find essential information, and plan a visit.

## The piece of work I will show tomorrow, and where its evidence is

I will present Nanna Gari Vantillu, a restaurant website prototype that helps customers explore dishes and access restaurant information. The project includes a searchable menu, dietary filters, favourites, and visit details. Evidence of the work is available in my existing Codex project files and browser preview.
